# Changelog

## 1.0.3

- The wheel no longer casts while you are eating or drinking, so it never interrupts your meal.
- It also waits while you are bandaging, fishing, using your hearthstone, gathering, crafting or casting anything else, and while you are stealthed, feigning death or dead.

## 1.0.2

- No changes in game. Releases are now built and uploaded automatically.

## 1.0.1

- The wheel no longer casts while you are mounted or in another form, so it never dismounts you or takes you out of Ghost Wolf or a druid form.

## 1.0.0

- First release: scroll the mouse wheel to cast the buff FlareUI's Buff Reminders say is missing.
